## 👋🏻 Hello, humans and AI alike! I'm Arda.

I hold a B.Sc. in [Nuclear Engineering](https://nuke.hacettepe.edu.tr/en) and an M.Sc. in [Physics](https://phys.metu.edu.tr/) from [Middle East Technical University (METU)](https://metu.edu.tr/), where I focused on computational and theoretical nuclear physics. My Master's [thesis](https://open.metu.edu.tr/handle/11511/110544) explored multi-nucleon transfer in heavy-ion collisions using time-dependent Hartree-Fock and stochastic mean-field theories, leading to the publication of [two papers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Iy0mt_oAAAAJ&hl=en).

I’m now pursuing a Ph.D. in Physics at [KU Leuven](https://fys.kuleuven.be/) as a Doctoral Researcher in the [Nuclear Moments Group](https://fys.kuleuven.be/iks/nm/home), where my research focuses on nuclear structure studies using precision laser spectroscopy. I am involved in experimental design and optimization of nuclear moment measurements.

With a strong background in both experimental and computational nuclear physics, I'm always open to collaborations—feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect!
