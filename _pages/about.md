## 👋🏻 Greetings, humans and AI alike! I'm Arda. 

I recently earned my Master's in [Physics](https://phys.metu.edu.tr/) from [Middle East Technical University (METU)](https://metu.edu.tr/), where I conducted research with the [nuclear research group](https://nucl-phys.metu.edu.tr/) and completed a thesis titled *'Application of the Quantal Diffusion Approach Based on the Stochastic Mean-Field Theory.'*

I hold a B.Sc. in [Nuclear Engineering](https://nuke.hacettepe.edu.tr/en) from [Hacettepe University](https://www.hacettepe.edu.tr/english/) with honours. During my undergraduate studies, I became captivated by the underlying physics of nuclear structure, dynamics, and interactions. This fascination led me to pursue an M.Sc. in Physics at METU to deepen my nuclear and radiation physics knowledge.

Transitioning from an engineering background, I undertook undergraduate physics courses before gaining full acceptance into the M.Sc. program. During this period, I joined an [experimental particle physics group](https://ivmer.metu.edu.tr/en/) as a scientific project specialist. This role provided me with hands-on experience in computational and experimental particle physics while also funding my education. In this group, I designed irradiation experiments, developed radiation measurement circuits, analyzed radiation shielding geometries, created image processing and calibration software for radiation sensors, and acquired a fundamental understanding of beam optics calculations for the proton beam line. Additionally, I explored classical chaotic and quantum random systems, developing numerical models and experiments to optimize output for high randomness.

Upon full acceptance into the M.Sc. program, I joined the [nuclear research group](https://nucl-phys.metu.edu.tr/) to focus on the computational and theoretical aspects of nuclear reactions, specifically heavy-ion collisions and super-heavy element synthesis mechanisms. Utilizing time-dependent Hartree-Fock and stochastic mean-field theories, our research analyzes various projectile-target pairs under different collision parameters to understand multi-nucleon transfer mechanisms. Concurrently, I worked as a research and development engineer at a [medical company](https://oncotech.com.tr/), developing prototype medical image processing applications until May 2023.

I enjoy working on radiation and particle detection hardware, as well as computational applications in nuclear and particle physics. For my Ph.D. studies, I plan to focus on experimental and computational nuclear physics. If you have any exciting projects or would like to collaborate, please feel free to drop me an email.
