## 👋🏻 Hello, humans and AI alike! I'm Arda.

I hold a B.Sc. in [Nuclear Engineering](https://nuke.hacettepe.edu.tr/en) with honours from [Hacettepe University](https://www.hacettepe.edu.tr/english/). My interest in the underlying physics of nuclear structure, dynamics, and interactions inspired me to pursue an M.Sc. in Physics at [Middle East Technical University (METU)](https://metu.edu.tr/), where I explored deeper into nuclear and radiation physics.

Transitioning from engineering to physics, I completed additional undergraduate physics courses before being fully accepted into the M.Sc. program. During this time, I worked as a scientific project specialist with an [experimental particle physics group](https://ivmer.metu.edu.tr/en/), gaining practical experience in both computational and experimental particle physics while supporting my studies. My role involved designing irradiation experiments, developing radiation measurement circuits, analyzing radiation shielding, and creating image processing software for radiation sensors. I also gained experience in beam optics calculations for a proton beamline and explored classical chaotic and quantum random systems to enhance randomness through numerical models and experiments.

Once I was fully enrolled in the M.Sc. program, I joined the [nuclear research group](https://nucl-phys.metu.edu.tr/), focusing on the computational and theoretical aspects of nuclear reactions, particularly heavy-ion collisions and super-heavy element synthesis. Through the application of time-dependent Hartree-Fock and stochastic mean-field theories, we explored multi-nucleon transfer mechanisms across various projectile-target pairs and published [two papers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Iy0mt_oAAAAJ&hl=en) on our findings. Simultaneously, I worked as a research and development engineer at a [medical technology company](https://oncotech.com.tr/), where I contributed to the development of prototype medical image processing applications until May 2023.

Recently, I earned my Master's degree in [Physics](https://phys.metu.edu.tr/) from METU, with a thesis titled [*'Application of the Quantal Diffusion Approach Based on the Stochastic Mean-Field Theory.'*](https://open.metu.edu.tr/handle/11511/110544).

My academic interests lie at the intersection of experimental and computational nuclear physics. I'm always open to collaboration and exciting projects—feel free to reach out via email if you'd like to connect!
